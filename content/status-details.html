What the proposed moratorium would do
  • Pause final school-closure decisions while examining alternatives.
  • Set transparent, districtwide criteria for future closures or consolidations.
  • Publish analyses of financial, enrollment, transportation, facilities, educational and equity impacts.
  • Compare reasonable alternatives before making irreversible decisions.
  • Ensure affected communities are heard before any future vote.

The request was submitted under California Education Code §35145.5. At the November 10 meeting, the Board did not advance it for substantive discussion or adopt the proposed resolution.

Earlier developments: September 2026

After substantial public input at Landers and Yucca Mesa, the immediate Landers closure decision was paused. Budget, enrollment, facilities and boundary questions remained open.

Z107.7 reported that at least 200 people attended the September 10 Landers meeting, where families, staff and residents challenged the consolidation case. Read the Z107.7 report.

Community questions included actual net savings, transportation, receiving-school expenses and which stabilization alternatives had been implemented first.
