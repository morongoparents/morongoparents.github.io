Update · Nov. 11, 2026

Board does not advance proposed school-closure moratorium.

At its November 10 meeting, the MUSD Board did not take up the proposed temporary closure and consolidation moratorium for substantive discussion or adopt the resolution, according to the community's account. The October 13 request followed delivery of more than 1,200 petition signatures. Landers remains open, but the Board has not adopted a formal closure moratorium.
